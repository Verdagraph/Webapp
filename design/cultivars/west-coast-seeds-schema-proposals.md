# West Coast Seeds import - Schema proposals

Importing West Coast Seeds' product pages produced 1,320 cultivars (see the [defaults README](../../packages/models/src/cultivars/defaults/README.md)). Their pages hold far more than the Cultivar model can store. This lists what was lost or bent to fit, and proposes fields to keep it. Counts are "crops" (one per distinct growing text, 175 in the sidecars) or "varieties" (1,133 child cultivars). The facts that did not fit are saved per cultivar in the `*.extras.json` sidecars, so they can be turned into attributes once a field exists.

Priority is a judgement of how much a planner feature would gain: **High** means the data is common and a planner calculation depends on it.

## Decisions (2026-10-20)

All of the agreed items below are implemented.

- **Agreed:** A1 (stored as `maturityDays` plus `maturityBasis`, with the Planner deriving harvest dates per origin), A2 (ranges), A3 (sun exposure set), A4 (propagation in the origin profile), A5 (sowing windows keyed by how a plant is started), A6 (spacing fields, size, and an ecology profile for root depth and nutrient role), A7 (unicode names, apostrophes allowed, up to 40 characters), A8 (organic and F1 are tags, not part of a name, and `traits` are called `tags`), B1 to B9, B11, B12 (as a default succession interval that the editor can override), B13 (renamed `WebResourceProfile`).
- **Category:** a `category` on the Cultivar, inherited from the crop, with uses such as cover cropping as tags.
- **Skipped:** B10 (colour names). **Out of scope:** B14 (packets and stock).
- **Defaults:** one merged collection, owned by nobody (a system collection), which every garden's own collection inherits.
- **Windows:** sowing windows are measured from the last frost and the first frost independently for each method, and replace the four frost date fields, which were removed. The Planner's PlantingWindows are generated from them.
- **Not done yet:** no source fills the ecology profile (root depth is not on seed packets). Which cultivars get planting windows is an interim rule; see [Cultivars in play](cultivars-in-play.md).

## C. Judgement calls made in the import

These were not in the source and are worth knowing when reading the data:

- Ranges are stored as midpoints. `sunExposure` is the first listed exposure.
- `germToFirstHarvest` subtracts the germination time from days to maturity when the figure counts from sowing, and adds the seedling age when it counts from transplant. For 10 crops whose page does not say what days to maturity counts from, it is assumed to be from sowing or transplant according to how the crop is normally grown (marked "assumed" in the sidecar).
- For transplanted crops with no stated seedling age, the age is estimated from the weeks-before-last-frost indoor start, only where that is given (pepper, eggplant, and a few others).
- `transplantable` is `false` for crops the page says to direct sow only, and `true` where the page allows starting indoors, even if it warns about root disturbance. Zinnia, phlox and a few others are `false` because the page says they transplant poorly.
- The parent tree follows how the store files its products, not botany. Bean groups Bush, Pole, Runner, Drying, Broad and Soya beans although they are different species or genera, Squash holds Zucchini and Pumpkin, and subtypes such as Cherry Tomato are market classes, not taxa. Parents therefore carry only attributes that are true of every member (Bean has none), and botanical classification belongs in the classification fields (B4), not in the parent chain.

## Suggested order

1. A1 (`maturityDays` and basis), B4 (lifecycle), A5 (sowing windows): these change what the Planner can calculate.
2. B7 (traits) and B8 (companions): large amounts of data, immediately useful in search and in the Layout.
3. Everything else as the features that need it are built.
